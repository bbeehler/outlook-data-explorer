About this data

Source: Société de l’assurance automobile du Québec (SAAQ), Véhicules en circulation on Données Québec, one file per year, licensed under CC BY 4.0. The SAAQ publishes each year’s file some time after the year ends; the latest available is shown.

Vehicles on the road means automobiles and light trucks authorised to circulate (SAAQ classes PAU and CAU: passenger vehicles up to 3,000 kg and vans, pickups and SUVs up to 4,000 kg). Personal use is class PAU (registered to people); business use is class CAU (registered to companies, governments, farms and the self-employed). Motorcycles, mopeds, motor homes, taxis, buses, heavy trucks and off-road vehicles are left out.

Fuel is recorded only from 2017. Unlike Ontario’s data, Quebec’s splits plug-in hybrids from battery electrics.

Makes and models come as the first five letters of the name, such as TOYOT or COROL. Full names shown are AIA Canada’s reading of those codes; the rest are shown as is. Regions are Quebec’s 17 administrative regions, based on the owner’s municipality. In 2021, La Haute-Yamaska and Brome-Missisquoi moved from Montérégie to Estrie, so those two regions jump that year.
