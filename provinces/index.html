About this data

Vehicles on the road: Statistics Canada, Table 23-10-0308-01, vehicle registrations by type of vehicle and fuel type, counted once a year from provincial and territorial registration records. New registrations: Statistics Canada, Table 20-10-0025-01, new motor vehicle registrations by fuel type, quarterly. Both under the Statistics Canada Open Licence.

Alberta: Alberta Transportation and Economic Corridors, registered motorized vehicles by fuel type and by body style as of March 31 each year, on open.alberta.ca (Open Government Licence – Alberta). Alberta’s own fuel categories differ from Statistics Canada’s.

Statistics Canada counts registrations, so a vehicle registered twice in a year or moved between provinces can affect the totals, and provinces count some vehicle types differently. Light vehicles are those weighing 4,536 kg or less: cars, SUVs and minivans (“multi-purpose vehicles”), pickups and vans.
