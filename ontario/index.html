About this data

Source: Ontario Ministry of Transportation, Vehicle population data, one file per year, each a snapshot as at December 31. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Ontario.

Vehicles on the road means vehicles the Ministry lists as fit with an active registration ("fit-active"). The figures cover passenger and commercial vehicles. Pickups and vans registered to a business are in the commercial class. Trailers, motorcycles, buses, off-road vehicles and snowmobiles are left out. The Ministry's 2015 file contains only a few dozen vehicles, so 2015 is skipped.

Makes and models come as Ministry codes, such as TOYT or RV4. Make names and the model names shown are AIA Canada's reading of those codes. Model codes without a clear match are shown as is, and one model can appear under more than one code.

Counties use the Ministry's boundaries, which predate municipal amalgamations: Metro Toronto is the City of Toronto, Ottawa-Carleton is the City of Ottawa, and Hamilton-Wentworth is the City of Hamilton. A small number of vehicles have no county.
