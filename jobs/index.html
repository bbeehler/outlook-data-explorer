About this data

Wages: Job Bank wages by occupation (Employment and Social Development Canada), hourly low (10th percentile), median and high (90th percentile), for Canada, each province and territory and their economic regions, on open.canada.ca. Where Job Bank can't publish a reliable wage for a small area, it's left blank.

Outlooks: Job Bank 3-year employment outlooks by province and economic region, with the reasons for each rating and worker counts. Job Bank reports a few neighbouring regions together (for example Montréal and Laval), so they show the same worker count (open.canada.ca).

Ten-year projections: the Canadian Occupational Projection System (COPS), Canada-wide job openings (new jobs, retirements and other replacement) and job seekers (school leavers, immigrants and others) by occupation, with ESDC's assessment of shortage or surplus (open.canada.ca). COPS is national only.

Workforce: Statistics Canada 2021 Census, tables 98-10-0449-01 and 98-10-0593-01: people in the labour force in each occupation (employed or looking for work), by age, gender and education; unemployment rate; and the share of workers who are self-employed. Census figures are randomly rounded, so small provinces can be blank or uneven.

Ontario detail: the Government of Ontario's labour market information job profiles add online job postings (Signal49 Research), employment change from the Labour Force Survey, in-demand skills and Ontario's skilled trades.
