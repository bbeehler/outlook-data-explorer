About this data

All figures come from the Government of Ontario's labour market information job profiles (Ministry of Labour, Immigration, Training and Skills Development), by 2021 National Occupational Classification (NOC) code. The ministry draws on several sources: projected job openings for 2024 to 2028 from Stokes Economics; annual online job postings from Signal49 Research (public job sites and company pages, not sites that require a login); employment change from Statistics Canada's Labour Force Survey; hourly wages from Job Bank; and the workforce profile (age, gender, education, unemployment) from the 2021 Census.

Employment change for a single occupation rests on a small Labour Force Survey sample and can swing sharply from year to year, so read the trend rather than any single year. Outlooks are the ministry's rating for 2025 to 2027.
